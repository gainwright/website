HOW TO REVIEW THE WEBSITE

  The page is deliberately short on first read. Every section has a fold
  marked "Open" with the detail behind it. The button at the top right,
  "Show all detail", opens every fold at once so you can read the whole page.
  The half-filled circle button next to it switches to the blueprint (dark) version.
  Section 01 has a calculator. Move the sliders and check the figures feel
  right to you; the maths is hours a week x 48 weeks x rate.
  Section 02 has the eight-question scorecard. Answer it and check the jobs
  it names make sense for the answers given.
  The forms (audit application, guide request, buy Ledgerwright) confirm
  on the page but do not send anywhere yet. That is wired up at launch.
